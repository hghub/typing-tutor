## Start with a correct payroll and income period

Lawful tax planning begins with what income is taxable, which year it belongs to, and what has already been withheld. A payment being genuine does not automatically make it deductible or creditable. Check payroll records, taxable benefits, bonus timing and withholding before considering a new financial product.

The [salary calculator](/tools/tax-calculator) is a salary-only base estimate before personal credits and filing adjustments. It does **not** currently calculate VPS or charity impact directly. The [Tax Credit Planner](/tools/tax-optimizer) is a separate, deliberately limited calculation for one reviewed contribution or donation at a time. Neither determines your personal eligibility or replaces a return preparation review.

## Separate deductions, credits and cash

A deduction changes taxable income under the relevant provision. A credit reduces tax using its own formula and conditions. Withholding is a tax payment, and a refund is a later settlement question. These are not interchangeable.

A pension contribution can restrict access to money. A donation permanently commits it to the recipient. A credit estimate does not undo that commitment or create money available for household spending. Review liquidity, product risk, fees and suitability independently of a tax illustration. Do not buy an unsuitable product merely to obtain a smaller potential tax reduction.

## Review current provisions rather than old product claims

Source checked **25 November 2026**: FBR's Income Tax Ordinance consolidated through **30 June 2026**. The planner selects **normal TY 2027, 1 July 2026–30 June 2027**; later changes and individual treatment still need review.

- **Section 63:** approved pension contributions use a qualifying-payment cap of 20% of taxable income in the limited current scenario. The amount of credit uses assessed tax divided by taxable income, multiplied by the qualifying capped payment. It is not a flat 20% saving or a universal PKR 500,000 allowance. Check eligible-person status, qualifying income, approved fund and evidence. Excluded scheme-balance transfers do not become qualifying payments just because they reach an individual pension account.
- **Section 61:** a qualifying donation has recipient, relationship, payment-year and evidence requirements. The individual contribution limit is 30% of taxable income for a non-associate-only scenario and 15% for an associate-only scenario. The consolidated cash-payment provision specifies a crossed bank cheque. Mixed relationships, non-cash gifts or other payment methods require separate assessment in this version.
- **Section 62:** the consolidation marks it omitted by Finance Act 2022. Historical footnotes showing insurance/share provisions do not make those provisions current. The planner no longer recommends life-insurance allocations as a section 62 credit.

These are contribution caps and conditions, not an eligibility decision or a promise of tax saved. Zakat, housing, senior/teacher treatment, special years, other credits and AOP-member adjustments are outside this prepared planner. Do not infer a benefit is unavailable in every case merely because this tool does not calculate it.

## Ask for the evidence before acting

For a real review, retain the relevant income period, assessed-tax basis, contribution or donation receipts, recipient/fund qualification, payment evidence and any professional advice privately. Do not upload them to a public repository, social post or website feedback. The calculator needs amounts and scenario assumptions, not identifying documents.

Have the reviewer explain A, B and C in the credit formula, contribution limits, any unused-credit restriction and how other credits/withholding interact. Section 65 generally does not allow unused Part X credit to be refunded or carried between years, subject to separate AOP-member provisions. A credit estimate and an actual withholding refund are different questions.

For a reproducible synthetic example, formula walkthrough and rounding limits, read [the Tax Credit Planner guide](/blog/decision-support/how-tax-shield-optimizer-helps-you-see-what-actually-saves-tax). Use the [official FBR consolidated Ordinance](https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/2026724177725705IncomeTaxOrdinanace2001.pdf) to verify sections 61, 62, 63 and 65 and their amendment notes. The consolidation/review dates are not claims that all provisions first became effective in 2026.
